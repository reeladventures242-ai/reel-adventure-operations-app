Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

Reel Adventure Tours uses the Reel Adventure Tours Operations app to manage bookings, trips, crew assignments, customer communications, and operational notifications.

Information We Collect

We may collect booking and operations information such as customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, trip dates, tour details, guest counts, payment status, crew assignments, WhatsApp message status, and email booking confirmations.

How We Use Information

We use this information to schedule tours, confirm bookings, coordinate captains and mates, send operational alerts, provide customer service, process trip-related communications, and maintain business records.

WhatsApp and Email Integrations

When connected, WhatsApp and Gmail integrations are used only for Reel Adventure Tours operations, including booking review, calendar updates, customer messages, crew alerts, and delivery status updates.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with authorized Reel Adventure Tours staff, vessel owners, captains, mates, service providers, or platforms needed to operate bookings and communications.

Data Security

We use access controls and secure hosted services to help protect operational data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information used by the operations app.

Data Retention

We retain booking, messaging, and operations records as needed for business operations, customer service, legal compliance, accounting, and safety records.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Reel Adventure Tours at reeladventures242@gmail.com.
